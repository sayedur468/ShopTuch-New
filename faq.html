Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding orders, international shipping, sizing, and care.

How does complimentary shipping work?

We offer complimentary standard courier shipping on all international orders over $75. Orders under $75 ship for a flat rate of $9.95. Priority Express air is available at checkout for $15.00.

What is your global return policy?

We provide a 30-day effortless return and exchange window on all unworn garments with original tags intact. We include prepaid return shipping labels inside every dispatched package.

How should I care for pure silk and wool garments?

For Mulberry silk blouses and dresses, we recommend delicate hand washing in cold water with pH-neutral detergent or eco-friendly dry cleaning. For structured wool overcoats, steam lightly and dry clean once per season.

How do I choose the correct garment size?

Our sizing follows standard European and American tailoring standards. Every product page contains a dedicated "Size & Fit Guide" with exact chest, waist, and hip measurements.
